The following is not an exhaustive list of projects.

- [**Framework for Modeling and Control of Networked Buildings:**](/projects-ICNB/) This work develops a scalable framework for modeling, estimation, simulation, and intelligent control of networked buildings for grid-edge applications. It includes control-oriented grey-box, black-box, and scientific-machine-learning models for residential and commercial building thermal dynamics; optimization- and Bayesian-inference-based state and parameter estimation; scalable building simulation and data-processing workflows; and building-grid co-simulation with electric distribution systems. The broader objective is to enable coordinated building-level and aggregator-level control strategies that improve building operation while supporting distribution-grid objectives.

- [**Intelligent Control of Residential Energy Systems:**](/projects-HEMS/) This work develops intelligent energy-management methods for residential energy systems under both grid-connected and outage conditions. It extends earlier home-energy-resiliency work into scalable residential-community simulation and control, incorporating photovoltaic generation, battery storage, HVAC systems, prioritized and controllable loads, model predictive control, reinforcement learning, and baseline and rule-based controllers. SmartCommunitySim provides the simulation and benchmarking environment for studying single-house and multi-house residential communities and comparing controller performance across on-grid and off-grid operating conditions.

- [**Power Systems Analysis Toolbox (PowerEdu.jl):**](/projects-PowerEdu/) A Julia-based power-systems analysis package developed to support Newton-Raphson power flow, continuation power flow, static state estimation, optimization, and power-system analysis workflows. The toolbox also includes ongoing development of power-system stability analysis and transient-simulation capabilities.

- [**Forecasting of Solar & Wind Energy:**](/projects-SWEEFA/) This work developed the Solar & Wind Energy Estimation and Forecasting Application (SWEEFA), a MATLAB GUI-based platform for plant-level solar and wind energy estimation and forecasting. The software includes weather and generation-data preprocessing, ANN- and ARIMA-based forecasting interfaces, and automated WRF-based numerical weather prediction using Bash scripts executed on a four-node Raspberry Pi 2 cluster.
